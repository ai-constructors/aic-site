Five functions. One platform.

PRECONSTRUCTION

Franklin reads, screens, and bids.

Franklin ingests incoming solicitations, runs Go/No-Go scoring, gathers documents, builds estimates and proposals, and drives bid packages to submittal — with your team approving every decision.

BACK OFFICE

The paperwork that eats your week.

Accounting and administrative agents handle recurring reconciliation, tracking, and reporting. Active on the platform now.

FIELD & PRODUCTION

Capture the field by voice or photo.

Log site conditions, inspections, and change events from the jobsite — hands-free — and route them straight into the project record. Active now.

BID INTELLIGENCE

Every bid makes the next one sharper.

Results feed a market-intelligence engine — competitor patterns, price-to-win, ROM calibration — that compounds with every project you run. In build.
