03 / About

About AIC.

AIC's founder and systems architect spent thirty-five years in construction before writing a line of this platform — every level of it, field through front office, across commercial, public works and federal contracting. He has helped start-ups find their footing, walked companies back from bankruptcy, and worked inside large corporate contractors where the systems were already in place. A background in human factors engineering shaped the rest: the tool should fit the person doing the job, not the other way around.

AIC is the work of downloading that career into something a contractor can actually run — a lifetime of practice woven together with AI agents built and tested against real jobs. It is not a concept deck. The system is field tested and operating in real time, with real contractors, today. We improve it continuously, keep it mission focused, and let the people using it decide what gets built next.
