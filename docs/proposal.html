Executive Summary & Introduction
PRE-2026-0047 · Rev 1

Cascade Industrial Builders is pleased to submit this Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) Proposal for the tenant improvement of the interior renovation of Sulphur Springs Public Library, located at 611 N. Davis Street, Sulphur Springs, Washington. This proposal is submitted in response to the invitation issued by Friends of the Library Inc. on June 4, 2026, and reflects the project scope described in the 100% Schematic Design documents prepared by Lone Star Design Group, dated June 10, 2026.

This GMP of $2,776,000 encompasses all general conditions, trade work, overhead, profit, insurance, bonding, and design contingency required to deliver a complete public library interior tenant improvement across 28,400 rentable square feet. The GMP is based on 100% SD documents; a final GMP confirmation will be issued upon receipt of 100% Construction Documents, currently anticipated July 7, 2026.

Relationship Note: Cascade Industrial Builders successfully completed Sulphur Springs Library Phase 1 (Floors 7–10) for Friends of the Library Inc. in Q4 2025, delivering on schedule and within budget. This proposal reflects our continued commitment to Friends of the Library's portfolio and our deep familiarity with building systems, base building MEP coordination, and the delivery team that made Phase 1 a success.

Qualifications, Exclusions & Clarifications
Read in conjunction with Estimate EST-2026-0047
Included in GMP: All general conditions; demolition and hazmat abatement allowance ($22,000); LGS framing and all interior finishes; aluminum storefront glazing; complete MEP systems including HVAC, plumbing rough-in, fire sprinkler extension, power and lighting, structured data cabling, access control, and AV rough-in; kitchen casework (3 units); custom reception millwork; toilet accessories and signage; final cleaning; and a 5% design contingency.
Excluded from GMP: Owner permit and plan check fees; OFCI items (AV displays, conference room equipment, appliances, furniture); structural modifications or seismic upgrades; any scope outside of Interior Renovation; design fees; escalation beyond June 30, 2026 lock (governed by escalation clause); work resulting from hidden conditions beyond the ACM survey scope.
Key Clarifications: (1) Working hours are Mon–Fri 7am–5pm; Saturday available with 48-hour notice at no premium charge for first 8 hours per day. (2) GMP is based on 100% SD; Cascade Industrial Builders reserves the right to adjust GMP by up to ±3% upon receipt of 100% CDs. (3) Millwork lead time is 8 weeks from approved submittal — shop drawing approval required by August 15, 2026 to maintain critical path.
