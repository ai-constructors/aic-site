04 / Questions

The things people ask first.

Is my data shared with other companies?

No. Your project data, estimates, and client information stay inside your engagement. Market-level bid outcomes inform an intelligence model in anonymized form only — never your raw data, never attributed.

Do I lose control of decisions?

No. Every consequential output is queued for a human to approve, edit, or reject before it acts. The agents do the work; your team owns the call.

What does it cost?

Pilots run on founding terms that we set with each company based on scope. The point of the cohort is a fair deal for early partners — reach out and we'll talk specifics. Outside the pilot, you can also buy à-la-carte modules or a custom system.

What if an agent makes a mistake?

That's exactly why the human-in-the-loop layer exists, and why we run live with close support during the pilot. You catch issues at review, and we fix the underlying behavior fast.

How long until something's useful?

The preconstruction agent is live today, so a pilot starts producing reviewable bid work in the first deployment, not months out.
